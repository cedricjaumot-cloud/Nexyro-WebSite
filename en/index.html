Our analysis method

Every property is reduced to a score, not a gut feeling.

We cross-reference dozens of criteria, from the property itself to its local context and the market, to keep only the projects that match your risk profile and your goal. There's no such thing as the perfect property: there's the property that matches yours.

The property

Performance and potential

  • Energy performance (EPC)
  • Short-term value creation potential (purchase, renovation)
  • Long-term value creation potential
  • Purchase price relative to the local market
Micro criteria

The local context

  • Population, density and average age of the neighbourhood
  • Local demographic pressure
  • Past and planned urban development
  • Ease or difficulty of resale in the area
Macro criteria

The market

  • Price trends over 10, 20 and 30 years
  • Rent trends in the area
  • Regional economic trends
  • Liquidity of the local market
For every project we retain, we build a file. It explains the property's potential and its points of attention, so your decision is informed, never imposed.
